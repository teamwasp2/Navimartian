Model notes

Traverse time, battery, and oxygen use a pressurized-rover force balance, not a fixed speed. Mars gravity is 3.72 m/s². The loaded rover is 2,500 kg. Rolling resistance is Crr = 0.085 + 0.14 × dust, so soft regolith sinks the wheels. The drivetrain is 82% efficient. Downhill, when gravity exceeds rolling resistance, 35% of that mechanical energy is recovered by regenerative braking. Cabin life support and thermal control draw a continuous 1.8 kW. Flat-ground speed is 2.4 m/s and is cut by dust, grade, and a 10 kW traction limit. Two crew consume 0.18 kg/h of oxygen on the flat, more on climbs and in dust. Map distance stays the horizontal step; time and energy use the true 3D path. A* still routes on slope, radiation, and dust. A storm raises dust inside its dome, which slows the rover and burns more battery without editing the terrain file.
